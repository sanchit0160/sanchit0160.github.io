A Quiet Tuesday Off Zara's Weekday Escape
The Titan Momo Monster Arrives

Tuesday, 6:47 AM. Zara, 20, is deep in her physical copy of "Can We Be Strangers Again". Her day off is sacred — no digital reminders, just sticky notes on the corkboard. But a giant, greasy Titan Momo Monster crashes in, splattering dumpling juice everywhere. Help Zara blast it with her Schezwan Blaster. Win, and Hakka Noodles + Veg Manchurian are delivered. Lose, and her book is ruined.

How to Play
01
Move
Desktop: WASD or ARROWS.
Mobile: Use the D-Pad, or simply swipe anywhere on the screen.
02
Attack
Desktop: SPACE to fire the Schezwan Blaster.
Mobile: Tap the FIRE button, or tap the screen. Each shot has a strict cooldown.
03
Defense
Dodge the flying greasy momos. Taking a hit grants a brief invincibility window — Zara blinks and cannot be damaged.
04
The Boss
The Titan Momo Monster becomes Enraged at 40% health — attacking faster and shooting more projectiles.
05
Win & Lose
Defeat the monster to enjoy Indian Chinese food. Lose, and your book gets ruined by greasy dumpling juice. You can Quit anytime during play using the button at the top-right of the game screen.
— ✦ —
Victory
Hakka Noodles & Veg Manchurian arrive.
Zara's book is safe. The Titan Momo Monster is reduced to a quiet puddle of sauce. She smiles, and adds a new sticky note to the corkboard: "Ordered Indian Chinese. Best Tuesday ever."
— ✕ —
Game Over
Your book is ruined by greasy dumpling juice.
The Momo Monster splattered all over "Can We Be Strangers Again". Zara sighs, and writes a new sticky note: "Buy a new book. Avoid momos forever."
